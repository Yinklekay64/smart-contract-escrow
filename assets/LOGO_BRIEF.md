# Logo brief

**Concept:** Trust + security + digital handshake. Escrow is a neutral,
transparent third party holding value until conditions are met — the logo
should read as *safe, decentralized, transactional*.

**Direction:** A geometric shield or vault icon combined with a linked-chain or
handshake motif, rendered in a flat, modern crypto/fintech style (thin strokes,
rounded geometric shapes, no gradient-heavy skeuomorphism).

**Color palette:** Deep navy/indigo `#0B1E3D` as primary, paired with a bright
teal `#00D1B2` accent for a trust + tech feel. Works on both light and dark
backgrounds.

**Format needs:** SVG (scalable, for README/website), plus exported PNG at
512×512 and a favicon-sized 32×32.

**Avoid:** Any existing brand's icon (no literal Ethereum diamond, no Stellar
logo reuse), no photorealistic imagery, no small text baked into the icon (keep
the icon separate from any wordmark).
